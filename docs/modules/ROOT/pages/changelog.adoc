= What's new
:description: What changed in FicstFluids, release by release.

What changed for players in each release, newest first.
If you are updating, read the *Known issues* of the release you are installing.

== 0.2.1 (Beta 1), 2026-11-22

First tester release.

=== Added

* Fluid networks are simulated by FicstFluids' own flow model instead of the game's.
During the beta the mod takes over only the networks you give it: `FF.TakeOver add all` takes every liquid network, and `FF.TakeOver add`, aimed at a network, takes that one (the way to add a gas network).
Whatever you connect to a held network becomes part of it; a new build connected to nothing held stays vanilla.
See xref:advanced.adoc#_running_networks[Running networks].
* Liquid and gas networks, with pumps, valves, Fluid Buffers, Industrial Fluid Buffers, train fluid platforms and fluid truck stations.
* Held networks keep their state through a save and load.
* Every log a tester sends names the build that wrote it (the first FicstFluids line).

=== Fixed (during testing, before this release)

* A Fluid Buffer left with nothing connected, or with only a valve on its pipe, is kept by the mod with its fluid, and survives a save and load.
Before, it went back to the game's flow.
* A Fluid Buffer on its own, or with only pipes on it, no longer shows its fluid as N/A.

=== Known issues

* `FF.TakeOver add all` takes liquid networks only.
Add each gas network on its own, aimed at it with `FF.TakeOver add`.
* A Fluid Buffer that is full when an edit rebuilds its network can take in flow it cannot hold.
A pump then shows flow (675 m³/min was seen) with every tank full, and the water is lost.
Until this is fixed, don't edit a network while one of its tanks is full.
